### [YouChina: China Entry Readiness Guide](https://www.you-china.com/en)
Free China entry-preparation guides for international visitors, covering visa-free and transit rules, arrival cards, airport-to-city transport, payments, eSIM/connectivity, maps and travel apps. It also maintains a public source-checking repository with official starting points and multilingual airport-arrival indexes.
